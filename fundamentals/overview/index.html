    Overview

    The image belows shows an overview over the Auto-LLM platform and it's pipeline.

    Based on the task (1) relevant to your use-case, you select a dataset (2) and the platform suggests a list of LLMs (3). The selected LLMs then get fine-tuned (4) using the training split of the chosen dataset. The energy consumed (5) during this fine-tuning gets recorded and can be viewed seperatly. After fine-tuning, all LLMs, with both the basic and the fine-tuned versions, get evaluated (6) on the unseen test-split of the chosen dataset, and a final overview enables you to see which model performs best on the given task and dataset.
